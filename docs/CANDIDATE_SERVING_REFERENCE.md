Next-meal candidates are evaluated at a reference serving instead of per 100 g (see `docs/CONFLICT_ENGINE_MODEL.md` §18a). A serving is a portion reported by the food's source record (`source_portion`) or the reference amount for its product category (`category_default`); otherwise values stay per 100 g and are labelled "serving unknown" (`none`). Servings only make foods comparable. They are not portion advice, and this educational prototype claims no labeling compliance.
