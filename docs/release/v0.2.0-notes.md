# ParkinSUM Companion v0.2.0 — Latest as of 2026-09-29

[GitHub release](https://github.com/albertzhzhou-droid/ParkinSUM/releases/tag/v0.2.0)
· [Latest release](https://github.com/albertzhzhou-droid/ParkinSUM/releases/latest)
· [Project showcase](https://albertzhzhou-droid.github.io/ParkinSUM/site/)

v0.2.0 brings the Paper workspace, evidence and replay tools, and refreshed
public showcase into one source release. It also versions the scoped
`@albertzhzhou-droid/parkinsum-companion` release-metadata package at `0.2.0`.
Flutter app metadata remains `0.2.0+2`.

**Educational research prototype only. Not medical advice, not a medical
device, and not a clinical decision tool. The model is not clinically
calibrated. Public demonstrations use synthetic or sample data only.**

## What changed

- **Paper workspace:** warm paper surfaces, bundled serif and sans-serif
  typography, responsive navigation, reduced-motion-aware transitions, and
  five chapters: Today, Timeline, Next meal, Insights, and Library. One entry
  composer and a shared command palette connect the main workflows.
- **Records with explicit provenance:** owner-entered observations,
  medication-intake dose evidence, portable record packages, recoverable
  history, and selected-record visit-preparation summaries preserve missingness
  and ownership boundaries.
- **Algorithm Observatory:** fixed synthetic scenarios, production-derived
  traces, configuration identities, explanation trees, bounded sensitivity
  views, and saved replay capsules make the checked behavior inspectable.
  Its 65 registered descriptors include 14 production-trace surfaces and
  51 static contracts; a descriptor is not executable verification coverage.
- **Evidence and research tools:** synthetic rule testing, rule-pack comparison,
  source/currency review, and development-only FHIR/CQL/CDS Hooks experiments
  keep unsupported, stale, ambiguous, or mismatched inputs explicitly held.
- **Public presentation:** updated README, documentation index, landing page,
  visual wiki, brand assets, and four Paper browser captures with synthetic
  state and capture provenance.

## Release contents

This release contains **source and release metadata**. GitHub source archives
and the versioned metadata package support repository review and citation.
The package is a metadata artifact, not the Flutter application, an algorithm
SDK, or a clinical library. No Android, iOS, desktop, or Web app binary is
distributed with this release; no app-store or backend deployment is included.

The [Paper screenshots](https://github.com/albertzhzhou-droid/ParkinSUM/blob/v0.2.0/docs/assets/screenshots/README.md) were captured from the
2026-09-29 development worktree using a fresh local profile, one synthetic
banana meal, zero selected medications, and no AI consent. They are rendering
evidence at the recorded capture revision, not final-tag binary attestations.

## Remaining limits

- **Five required evidence lanes remain incomplete:** uncertainty assessment,
  human factors, scientific validation, model qualification, and independent
  review. CoU ledger `2026.09.29-v39` matches the current identities, but its
  release disposition remains `blockedPendingEvidence` and research-trace
  promotion remains blocked. Mechanical identity integrity supplies no new
  empirical evidence or independent approval.
- **Complete result-dependency closure remains held:** the current disposition
  is `root_manifest_resolved_closure_held`. Registered roots and bounded source
  checks do not establish complete transitive result-dependency coverage.
- **Fourteen known transitive development-dependency alerts remain:** six high
  and eight moderate, refreshed after the main-branch dependency scan on
  2026-09-29. The production-only npm audit excludes these development
  tools; its zero known production vulnerabilities must not be presented as
  zero repository alerts. No broad dependency upgrade is part of this release.
- **The mechanistic model remains `trace_only`:** its educational traces do
  not select meal times or reorder candidate recommendations. Optional,
  consented loopback AI is limited to screened non-`BLOCK` candidate reranking
  and wording polish; it cannot alter deterministic rules or safety gates.
- **Further review remains open:** independent scientific and human review,
  physical-device acceptance, general standards conformance, and unresolved
  third-party model/data/clinical-content licensing are not established by
  synthetic tests or this publication.
