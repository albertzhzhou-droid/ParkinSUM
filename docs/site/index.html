I. The everyday workspace

Five chapters.
A clear place for each task.

Paper pairs warm opaque surfaces with readable typography and a responsive sidebar. The app's finite transitions respect reduced motion settings.

01 / Today

Start with an entry.

One composer for a meal, medication intake, or observation, alongside recent activity and previews.

02 / Timeline

Keep the sequence.

Browse records, inspect details, edit entries, and choose explicit export actions.

03 / Next meal

Compare with context.

Set a time window and inspect candidate explanations, missing information, and model limitations.

04 / Insights

Read your records.

Explore descriptive logging and protein-distribution views, without inferring clinical outcomes.

05 / Library

Check the source.

Review selected medications and the food/medication catalog with source and jurisdiction context.

Evidence, data, and operational tools are reachable from the sidebar or ⌘K / Ctrl+K. Local AI configuration lives in Settings → Advanced.

Inspect capabilities & limits ↗
The mechanistic model remains trace-only.

Its educational curves do not choose a meal time or reorder recommendations. Optional consent-gated loopback AI may only rerank rule-screened, non-BLOCK candidates or polish existing wording; it cannot override rule-owned decisions, scores, evidence, or safety gates.

Reference dataset

USDA FoodData Central, release 2025-04-24

Supports
Official nutrient ids, units and conversion factors; 26,995 SR Legacy rows for 280 foods, each re-read from the source CSV in tests.
Does not support
Personal intake, tolerance or a food choice for any specific person.
Source id
src.usda.fdc.full_download.2025_04_24

Reference dataset

USDA SR Legacy, April 2018

Supports
Paired raw/cooked, whole/part and fresh/dried records for consistency checks between foods.
Does not support
Every food a person eats: values are sample averages that describe foods, not people.
Source id
src.usda.sr_legacy.2018

Consensus standard

FAO Food and Nutrition Paper 77

Supports
General energy factors used to reconcile reported energy with the macronutrients.
Does not support
Proof of an error: a residual only flags a record for review within a tolerance.
Source id
src.fao.food_energy.2003

Primary human study

Nutt et al. 1989, plasma LNAAs

Supports
Naming LNAA competition as a studied mechanism in the competition trace.
Does not support
A universal penalty or a predictor. On a normal diet most participants showed no important LNAA effect, and the trace keeps that negative finding.
Source id
src.nutt.lnaa.1989

Analytical study

Duan et al. 2021, faba bean L-dopa

Supports
Food-borne L-dopa shown as presence and variability context.
Does not support
A cooked-serving amount, an absorbed amount or any dose equivalent.
Source id
src.duan.faba_ldopa_thermal.2021

Primary human study

Hallberg et al. 1991, calcium and iron

Supports
A co-consumption context note: single-meal isotope studies of calcium and non-heme iron.
Does not support
Long-term iron status or an instruction about food or medicines.
Source id
src.hallberg.calcium_iron.1991

Every model assumption points to one of these records. A record states what it supports and what it does not.

III. The record · October 2026

Data you can trace.
Changes you can audit.

Food values are copied from official sources and tested back against them. Every change to the model's configuration appends a ledger record that stays blocked until evidence exists.

26,995
official FoodData Central rows for 280 foods, each re-read from the source CSV in tests
148
nutrients per record, from individual sugars to 33 fatty-acid chain lengths
25
disagreements inside the source records, pinned in tests and never corrected
0.2667
median competing-LNAA fraction of protein across 140 records (the old reference was 0.45)

A source-record disagreement on a value the conflict engine uses adds an uncertainty reason and caps confidence. It never changes a score. Values describe foods, not people.

A boundary, not a footnote

For educational review.

ParkinSUM is not a medical device or clinical decision tool. Its model is not clinically calibrated. Do not use it for diagnosis, treatment, medication timing, dietary guidance, patient care, or emergencies.

Public demonstrations use synthetic or sample data. Owner-entered records and exports may contain sensitive information and must stay out of public media.
